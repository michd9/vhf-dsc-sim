DSC log

Press MENU to place an individual call or set the distress nature. Other stations call you now and then. Open the distress cover and hold the key for 3 seconds to send an alert.

International channels

Tap a row to tune the radio. Duplex channels are for coast stations: the ship transmits on the lower frequency and listens 4.6 MHz higher. Ch 70 is DSC only and never carries voice.
